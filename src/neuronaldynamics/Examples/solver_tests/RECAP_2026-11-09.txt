1. MAIN RESULT
--------------
All IK network references (1 pop Monte Carlo and 3 pop networks) used Euler-Maruyama with MC_DT = 0.01 ms and spikes
detected on the grid. They were converged in N but NOT in dt:
- 3 pop, sigma = 1 mV, step: the post-stimulus volley of exc 2 (~118-135 ms) and the inhibitory bursts it triggered
  are an Euler artefact. They shrink/disappear for smaller dt. The volleys of the first 100 ms move later by ~3 ms.
- Euler converges very slowly: the burst times still move by 0.2-0.3 ms per halving of dt below 0.0025 ms; only at
  dt = 0.0003125 ms does it agree with the converged solution (0.8 %).
- Stochastic Heun with interpolated spike times (new, method='heun') is converged at dt = 0.01 ms (0.9-2.4 % between
  dt = 0.01 ... 0.000625 ms, one outlier of 11 % at 0.0025 ms: a 0.6 ms shift of one burst).
- 20 Hz sine, sigma = 1 mV: old reference 15 % off. 1 pop and sigma = 3.16 mV: old references 1.6-2.8 % off (fine).

The 2D solver produced the (non-existent) event through the numerical diffusion of the SG flux in v. With FCT
(new, flux_limiter='fct') or dv = 0.05 mV the event is gone.

- two-node closure + FCT within 3 % in 5 of 6 cases; in the hardest case 12 % = inhibitory bursts 0.8 ms early.
- 2D + FCT converged in du (du 2 vs 1: 5 %) and dv (dv 0.1 vs 0.05 at du 2: 0.095 vs 0.090); best model in the
  hardest case (4.9 %, burst times within 0.04 ms). It is the closure-free control.
- FCT does not change the 2D solution for 1 pop at sigma = 2 mV; it helps where populations synchronise.
- two-node closure on dv = 0.05 mV in the hardest case: 0.117 (dv 0.1: 0.124), inhibitory bursts still 0.6 ms early
  (centroid 78.15 vs 78.77 ms): the remaining 12 % is closure error, the 2D solution gets the bursts right.
- MIIND / refractory density (alternatives 4a, 4b of the last recap) are no longer needed for the threshold event.
